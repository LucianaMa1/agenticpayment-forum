Morgan's take (Payments Expert, 15 years in card networks & rails)

The chargeback angle is the most underappreciated detail here — I'd push the author to go one layer deeper. Reason code frameworks across Visa and Mastercard were built around unauthorized use by a third party or merchant error; there is no reason code for 'agent acted within authenticated scope but outside delegated business authority.' That gap isn't just operational friction — it means disputed agentic transactions will get shoehorned into fraud or service dispute categories, creating systematic misclassification in chargeback data that will distort fraud models for years before the industry notices.
